🏎️ Drag Race Game 🏁

Race to the finish line and stop accurately!

🎯 Challenge: Timing & Control

Learn to control speed and stop at the right moment!

0 0

🎮 Controls

⬆️

UP ARROW

Hold to accelerate!

Go faster and faster!

⬇️

DOWN ARROW

Hold to brake!

Slow down to stop!

📖 How to Play

  • 1️⃣ Press SPACE to start the countdown
  • 2️⃣ When "GO!" appears, hold UP ARROW to accelerate
  • 3️⃣ Watch for the FINISH LINE approaching
  • 4️⃣ Press DOWN ARROW to brake before the finish
  • 5️⃣ Stop in the GREEN ZONE for maximum points!

🏆 Scoring

Perfect Stop (Green Zone): Up to 1000 points!

Overshot: Fewer points - slow down earlier!

Too Early: No points - you didn't reach the finish!
