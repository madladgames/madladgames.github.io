How to Play

  • 🔧 First, build your race car — pick the body, color, engine, tires, and spoiler
  • 🏎️ Your car accelerates automatically — it goes really fast!
  • ⬅️➡️ Use arrow keys to steer left and right, and down arrow to brake
  • ⚠️ Dodge barriers, cones, oil slicks, and other race cars!
  • 💥 3 crashes and your car breaks apart!
  • 🔨 Mash the SPACE BAR to put your car back together and keep racing
  • 🏁 Complete all laps to finish the race — can you get 1st place?
