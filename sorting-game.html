🚗 Sorting Game 🐶

Sort items into the right categories!

🎓 Learning: Categorization

This game teaches toddlers to recognize and sort items into groups!

🎯 Where Do They Go?

🏠

GARAGE

Vehicles belong here!

🚗 🚕 🚌 🏎️ 🚒 🚂 ✈️ 🚲
🌳

GRASS

Land animals belong here!

🐶 🐱 🐰 🦊 🐻 🦁 🐴 🦌

📖 How to Play

  • 1️⃣ Look at the items at the top of the screen
  • 2️⃣ Drag each item with your finger or mouse
  • 3️⃣ Drop vehicles 🚗 into the Garage 🏠
  • 4️⃣ Drop animals 🐶 into the Grass 🌳
  • 5️⃣ Sort all items correctly to win! 🎉

🧠 Why Sorting is Important

🔍 Observation: Sorting helps kids notice similarities and differences between objects!

🎯 Categorization: Learning to group things is a key cognitive skill!

📚 Vocabulary: Kids learn words like "vehicles" and "animals"!

🧩 Problem Solving: Deciding where each item goes builds thinking skills!
