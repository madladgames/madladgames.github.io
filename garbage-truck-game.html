🚛 Garbage Truck Game 🗑️

Drive around the neighborhood and collect all the trash!

🎮 No Controller - Use Keyboard

🎮 Bluetooth Controller

  • Left Stick Steer the truck
  • RT/R2 Accelerate (forward)
  • LT/L2 Reverse
  • A/B Collect trash

💡 Connect your controller via Bluetooth, then press any button to activate it!

⌨️ Keyboard Controls

  • ↑ / W Drive forward
  • ↓ / S Reverse
  • ← / A Turn left
  • → / D Turn right
  • Space Collect trash

🗑️ How to Play

  • 1️⃣ Drive your garbage truck through the neighborhood streets
  • 2️⃣ Find the trash cans placed near houses
  • 3️⃣ Drive close to a trash can and press the action button to collect it
  • 4️⃣ Watch the truck's arm extend and grab the trash! ♻️
  • 5️⃣ Collect all 10 trash cans to complete your route! 🎉
