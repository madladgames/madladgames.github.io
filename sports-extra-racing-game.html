How to Play

  • 🏎️ Pick your car class and color in the garage
  • 🛣️ Race around a winding road with sharp curves and S-bends!
  • 🚀 Your car accelerates automatically — it goes REALLY fast!
  • ⬅️➡️ Use arrow keys to steer left and right, down arrow to brake
  • ⚠️ Watch for curve warnings — steer into the turn to stay on the road!
  • 🚧 Dodge cones, oil slicks, barriers, and other cars!
  • 💥 3 crashes and your car is wrecked!
  • 🔨 Mash the SPACE BAR to repair your car and keep racing
  • 🏁 Complete 3 laps to finish — can you get 1st place?
