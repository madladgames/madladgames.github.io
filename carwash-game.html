🚗 Car Wash Sequence Game 🧽

Learn the correct order to wash a car!

🎓 Learning: Sequences & Order

This game teaches toddlers the importance of doing things in the right order!

🌟 The Correct Washing Order 🌟

💧
Step 1
RINSE
Wet the car first!
🧴
Step 2
SOAP
Apply soap!
🧽
Step 3
SCRUB
Scrub the dirt!
💧
Step 4
RINSE
Rinse off soap!

📖 How to Play

  • 1️⃣ Look at the step indicators at the top - they show what to do next!
  • 2️⃣ Click the correct tool button at the bottom (Water, Soap, or Scrubber)
  • 3️⃣ Click on different parts of the car to wash them!
  • 4️⃣ Wash ALL parts of the car before moving to the next step
  • 5️⃣ Follow the correct order: Rinse → Soap → Scrub → Rinse

🤔 Why Does Order Matter?

💧 First Rinse: Removes loose dirt so we don't scratch the car when scrubbing!

🧴 Soap: Helps lift and break down the sticky dirt!

🧽 Scrubbing: Works with soap to clean off all the dirt!

💧 Final Rinse: Washes away all the soap and dirt, leaving the car sparkly clean!
