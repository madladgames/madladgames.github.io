How to Play

  • 🏎️ Pick your rally class and livery color in the garage
  • 🏔️ Every car comes with a BIG SPOILER for maximum downforce!
  • 🚀 Your car accelerates automatically — it goes REALLY fast!
  • ⬅️➡️ Use arrow keys to steer left and right, down arrow to brake
  • 🏔️ Race through different terrain stages — gravel, dirt, mud, snow, and tarmac!
  • ⚠️ Dodge rocks, logs, hay bales, puddles, and other rally cars!
  • 💥 3 crashes and your car is wrecked!
  • 🔨 Mash the SPACE BAR to repair your car and keep racing
  • 🏁 Complete all stages to finish the rally — can you get 1st place?
