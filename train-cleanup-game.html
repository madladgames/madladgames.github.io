🚂 Train Cleanup 🌿

Drive the train through the maze and clean up fallen branches!

🎯 Mission: Clean the Tracks!

Collect all the fallen branches and dump them at the garbage dump!

🎮 Controls

⬅️

LEFT ARROW

Turn left at junctions

⬆️

UP ARROW

Drive the train forward!

Hold to keep moving

➡️

RIGHT ARROW

Turn right at junctions

📖 How to Play

  • 1️⃣ Press ⬆️ UP to make the train go forward along the tracks
  • 2️⃣ At junctions (where tracks split), press ⬅️ LEFT or ➡️ RIGHT to choose which way to go
  • 3️⃣ Drive over the 🌿 fallen branches to pick them up into the cargo car
  • 4️⃣ Collect all 5 branches scattered around the maze
  • 5️⃣ Drive to the 🗑️ DUMP at the bottom-right to dump the branches! 🎉

💡 Tips

  • 🌟 The train follows the tracks automatically on curves and straight sections
  • 🌟 Yellow arrows show which way you'll go at junctions - change with LEFT/RIGHT
  • 🌟 Branches glow yellow so they're easy to spot
  • 🌟 The DUMP glows when all branches are collected!
