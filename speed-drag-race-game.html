🏎️ Speed Drag Race 🏁

Choose your car and race to the gantry!

🎯 Challenge: Speed & Reflexes

Hold the gas and race your car past the gantry finish line!

🎮 Controls

⬆️

UP ARROW / Touch

Hold to accelerate!

Go faster and faster to win!

SPACE

SPACE BAR

Start the race!

Also works for restart

📖 How to Play

  • 1️⃣ Choose your car - Green Convertible, VW Beetle, or Muscle Car!
  • 2️⃣ Press SPACE or click "Start Race" to begin
  • 3️⃣ Watch the Christmas tree lights count down - amber, amber, amber, GREEN!
  • 4️⃣ Hold ⬆️ UP ARROW (or touch the screen) to go fast!
  • 5️⃣ Race past the finish gantry before the other cars! 🏆
