🦋 Butterfly Match 🌸

Help butterflies find their matching flowers!

🎓 Learning: Shapes & Colors

This game teaches toddlers to match by color and shape — building pattern recognition skills!

🎯 Match the Butterfly to its Flower!

🎨

COLORS

Match the butterfly's color to the flower!

🟢 Green 🩷 Pink 🔵 Blue 🟠 Orange 🟣 Purple
🔷

SHAPES

Match the shape on the butterfly's wings too!

⭕ Circle ⭐ Star ❤️ Heart 💎 Diamond 🔺 Triangle

📖 How to Play

  • 1️⃣ Look at the butterflies 🦋 flying at the top
  • 2️⃣ Each butterfly has a color and a shape on its wings
  • 3️⃣ Find the flower 🌸 with the same color and shape
  • 4️⃣ Drag the butterfly to its matching flower
  • 5️⃣ Match all butterflies to win and go to the next level! 🎉

🧠 What Kids Learn

🎨 Color Recognition: Identifying and naming different colors!

🔷 Shape Identification: Learning circles, stars, hearts, diamonds, and triangles!

🧩 Pattern Matching: Finding items that share two attributes at once!

🎯 Fine Motor Skills: Dragging and dropping builds coordination!

🌱 Progressive Difficulty: Each level adds more butterflies to match!
